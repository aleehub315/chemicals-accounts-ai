# AI Commands

The AI Agent should understand commands such as:

1. Customer balance
Example:
"Customer CUST-001 ka balance batao"

2. Customer payment
Example:
"CUST-001 se 50,000 payment receive hui"

3. Sales entry
Example:
"CUST-001 ko PVA 100 KG 780 per KG sale hua"

4. Purchase entry
Example:
"PVA 200 KG 700 per KG purchase hua"

5. Expense entry
Example:
"Transport expense 5,000 add karo"

6. Stock
Example:
"PVA ka current stock batao"

7. Commission
Example:
"Meri commission calculate karo"

8. Reports
Example:
"Aaj ki sales report batao"

9. Account balance
Example:
"Company ka current cash balance batao"
